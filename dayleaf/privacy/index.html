Dayleaf privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Dayleaf is a reading app by Kraftleaf Studios. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it contains no tracking code.

What stays on your device

Your books, covers, highlights, notes, drawings, vocabulary, collections, reading-time statistics and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The app never uploads them. Backups you create are files you save yourself, to a place you choose.

What leaves your device

  • Dictionary lookups. When you tap Define on a word, the app sends that single word to the public dictionary service api.dictionaryapi.dev over HTTPS to fetch the definition. No other information is sent. If you choose Search the web, your browser opens a Google search for that word.
  • Purchases. If you buy Dayleaf Pro, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive only confirmation that the purchase is valid. We never see your payment details.
  • Android system backup. If Android backup is enabled on your device, the system may include some of the app's data in your Google backup, under Google's terms.

Files you open

Books you import are copied into the app's private storage. The original file is never modified. Annotated PDF exports are written only where you choose to save them.

Children

Dayleaf is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stores on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
